Engineering the Gateway: Reconstructing the Kemayoran Main Terminal Geometry

With the spatial footprint and historical charts established, the next phase of the project focuses on structural execution: bringing the Kemayoran Airport Main Terminal (WIID) into modern digital creation pipelines.

Recreating a historic landmark for real-time environments requires a delicate balance between visual accuracy and rendering performance. Rather than producing a heavy, unoptimized CAD dump, the terminal asset was constructed specifically for cross-platform flight simulation and interactive engine deployment.

Technical Specifications & Multi-Engine Pipeline Architecture

To ensure the asset integrates seamlessly across platforms — from custom game engines to dedicated flight simulators

The architecture follows a standardized PBR asset pipeline:

  • Multi-Simulator Compatibility: Optimized geometric topology engineered for immediate conversion and placement via X-Plane WorldEditor (WED), Microsoft Flight Simulator SDK, Prepar3D, and FlightGear.
  • PBR Material Workflow: Fully textured utilizing high-quality Physically Based Rendering maps (Albedo, Normal, Roughness, Metallic/Specular) to guarantee realistic material response under dynamic atmospheric and daylight lighting.
  • Performance Topology: Clean, uniform low-poly polygonal structure designed to preserve draw calls and frame rates during complex flight operations.
  • Complete UV Unwrapping: Clean, non-overlapping UV layouts ready for custom lightbaking or secondary detail maps.

Standardized Format Package

To fit diverse pipeline workflows without conversion friction, the asset bundle includes both raw source and production-ready exchange formats:

  • Native & Source Files: Blender (.blend), Autodesk FBX (.fbx), Wavefront (.obj / .mtl).
  • Real-Time & Web Exchange: glTF / GLB (.gltf, .glb), Alembic (.abc), Stanford (.ply).
  • Texture Archive: Compressed archive containing all PBR maps and high-resolution PNG image textures.
Production Note: This asset focuses on structural exterior fidelity and architectural accuracy. It is delivered static and unrigged, serving as a clean anchor for airport scenery placement, environment staging, and flight sim scenery packages.
