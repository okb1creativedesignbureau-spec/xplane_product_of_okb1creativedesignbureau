1. Forgotten Infrastructure: The Origins of Lanud Gorda

Located in the Banten province west of Jakarta (Tangerang area), Lanud Gorda (Gorda Airfield) represents a unique chapter in Indonesian military aviation history.

Originally cleared and constructed in 1942 by Japanese occupation forces during World War II, Gorda was built as a tactical dispersal strip designed to support imperial fighter and light bomber operations away from primary West Java airbases like Kalijati and Tjililitan (Halim).

Following the war and throughout the post-Cold War era, Gorda was never developed into a major paved military base or commercial hub. Instead, it was maintained under the administrative purview of the Indonesian Air Force (TNI-AU), serving two primary roles:

  • Parachutist Drop Zone (DZ): Functioning as a primary open grass landing ground for airborne tactical training, combat jump exercises, and supply drop operations.
  • Tactical Tactical Airfield Training: Periodically utilized by TNI-AU Lockheed C-130 Hercules transports for rough-field / unpaved strip tactical landing and takeoff drills.

3. WorldEditor (WED) Restoration: Hand-Crafting Tropical Rice Paddies

Default X-Plane 12 scenery terrain rendering algorithms typically depict rural Indonesian airfields as flat, uniform green grassland or low-resolution satellite textures. To accurately recreate the atmospheric environment of Banten, Gorda was reconstructed from scratch using custom terrain techniques in WED:

  1. Manual Paddy Field Reconstruction: Rather than relying on automated simulator landclass, the surrounding agricultural landscape was hand-polygoned in WED to faithfully recreate authentic Southeast Asian wet-rice paddies (sawah), complete with drainage channels, earth dikes, and varying crop saturation colors.
  2. Tactical Rough-Field Geometry: Recreates the unpaved grass runway corridor, perimeter tree lines, and local military boundary markers matching ground-truth satellite positioning.
  3. Period Aircraft Staging: Perfect for historical ops — from staging WWII Allied/Japanese bombers (like a Wellington or Nell sitting right on the edge of the paddy fields) to modern TNI-AU tactical air mobility sorties.
