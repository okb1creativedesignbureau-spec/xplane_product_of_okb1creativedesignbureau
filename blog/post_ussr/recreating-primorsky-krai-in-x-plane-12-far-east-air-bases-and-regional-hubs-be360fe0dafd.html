Recreating Primorsky Krai in X-Plane 12: Far East Air Bases and Regional Hubs

The Russian Far East presents one of the most operationally challenging and historically rich aviation environments in modern flight simulation. Bordered by the Sea of Japan and characterized by dense taiga, rugged coastal terrain, and unpredictable maritime weather, the Primorsky Krai region relies heavily on aviation infrastructure to connect isolated communities and support critical defense networks.

From massive post-Soviet Naval Aviation strike bases to remote bush strips along the Sikhote-Alin mountains, this region requires precise, low-altitude flying and strict atmospheric navigation.

To bring this vast region to life in X-Plane 12, a progressive early-access scenery project is underway to recreate Primorsky Krai’s network of military air bases, civil regional airfields, and strategic heliports.

Key Historical & Operational Hubs in the Region

Primorsky Krai has long served as a key front-line operational zone for Soviet and Russian Naval Aviation (AV-MF) and the Air Force (VKS), alongside vital regional air transport networks:

  • Strategic Naval Aviation & Bomber Hubs: Bases like Vozdvizhenka and Khorol historically hosted long-range heavy bombers, including Tu-22M3 Backfires and Tu-95RTs Bear-Ds, serving as primary deterrent bases facing the Pacific theater.
  • Interceptor Defense Intersections: Facilities like Tsentralnaya Uglovoye and Galenki operated quick-reaction interceptor squadrons (MiG-31, Su-27) tasked with air defense across the Maritime Territory.
  • Remote Transport Lifelines: Regional strips such as Kavalerovo, Dalnegorsk, and Plastun connect small coastal communities where overland transit remains seasonal or impossible due to extreme winter conditions.

Included in the Current Early Access Build

The project is structured around a rolling update model, expanding coverage across airfield clusters as assets and terrain layouts are completed. The current build includes immediate access to the following airfields and vertical flight hubs:

Active Airfields & Aerodromes

  • Okeanskaya (Starry / Severny) Aerodrome: A key general aviation and light transport hub situated on the outskirts of Vladivostok.
  • Maikhe Airfield: Regional field setup supporting utility flight operations.
  • Novorossiya Aerodrome: Operational airfield hub optimized for regional transport and local traffic.
  • Ozyornye Klyuchi (Lake Springs) Airfield Expansion: Enhanced coverage expanding operational boundaries near the main regional transport hubs.

Operational Heliports & Specialized Helipads

  • Vladivostok Old Airport Heliport: Restored vertical-lift hub situated on historic civil airport grounds.
  • Cape Markovsky Heliport: Coastal maritime helipad layout testing low-margin approaches under heavy fog.
  • Mt. Varginara Heliport: High-elevation ridge helipad demanding precise power management.
  • Avialift Vladivostok Heliport: Dedicated commercial and utility helicopter operational base.

Recreating the Far East Environment in X-Plane 12

Building sceneries specifically for X-Plane 12 allows for full utilization of the engine’s dynamic environment:

  • Volumetric Weather Dynamics: Experience sudden coastal fog banks, low-level icing, and severe wind shear off the Sikhote-Alin mountain range.
  • Photometric Lighting & PBR Materials: Night intercept missions out of Uglovoye or low-visibility helicopter extractions at Cape Markovsky benefit from realistic runway lighting and surface wetness reflections.
  • Seasonal Ground Surface Rendering: High-latitude winter conditions alter landing performance across unpaved regional grass strips like Agzu and Kamenka.
