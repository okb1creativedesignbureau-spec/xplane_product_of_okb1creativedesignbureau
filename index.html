The Vision & Hangar Ethos

OKB-1 is an independent solo-developer design bureau specializing in high-fidelity X-Plane mod products, focusing specifically on custom sceneries and precision orthophotos projects. Every asset is engineered from first-principles based on authentic architectural layouts, historical blueprints, and real-world performance parameters rather than decorative visual meshes.

By developing and hosting independently (or me as indie dev working solo... lol...), we maintain total ownership over our work, bypass restrictive platform curation, and deliver direct access to serious flight simulation enthusiasts.
