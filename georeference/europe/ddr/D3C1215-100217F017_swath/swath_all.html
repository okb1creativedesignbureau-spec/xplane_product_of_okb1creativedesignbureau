Bundle Description

This complete multi-swath package consolidates all ten georeferenced segments (Swath A through Swath J) of declassified USGS KH-9 HEXAGON reconnaissance roll D3C1215-100217F017 (captured 10 April 1979). Covering a continuous Cold War trans-border corridor from the western approaches around Bad Bodenteich and Salzwedel across Rathenow, Greater Berlin, Frankfurt (Oder), Słubice, and Rzepin to Świebodzin and Lake Lubinieckie, the collection provides seamless regional coverage for GIS analysis, historical cartography, and flight simulation scenery development.

Every raster in the bundle (D3C1215-100217F017_a.tif through D3C1215-100217F017_j.tif) is rectified in EPSG:25833 with a Lanczos 6×6 resampling kernel using Thin Plate Spline (TPS) transformations backed by 3,282 verified ground control points across the ten individual QGIS georeferencing reports.
