Product Description

Swath E covers the pre-Berlin western approach swath segment of declassified KH-9 HEXAGON reconnaissance roll D3C1215-100217F017 (captured 10 April 1979), spanning Nauen, Falkensee, Brieselang, and Wustermark along the western corridor approaching Greater Berlin. Carefully aligned ground control points and high-order spline rectification prepare the historical raster for GIS analysis, archival research, and flight/scenery reference workflows.

All raster georeferencing for this product is executed with a Lanczos 6×6 kernel using the Thin Plate Spline (TPS) transformation parameters documented in D3C1215-100217F017_e_report.pdf.
