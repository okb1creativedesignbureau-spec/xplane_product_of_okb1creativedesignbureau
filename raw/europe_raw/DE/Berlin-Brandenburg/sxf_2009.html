🌍 Unearthing Berlin Schönefeld at the Crossroads of Transition

Long before Berlin Brandenburg Airport (BER) opened its doors to global traffic, the land surrounding Schönefeld (SXF) was a complex patchwork of Cold War aviation history, East German transport infrastructure, and massive ongoing earthworks. In 2009, aerial survey planes captured a rare transitional snapshot of EDDB: the northern runway system was handling heavy low-cost and charter carrier traffic, while the mega-terminal south of the airfield was just taking physical shape in the sand and concrete.

This dataset provides raw, uncompressed 1:500 scale georeferenced raster coverage compiled directly from official GeoBasis-BB survey maps. Designed primarily for GIS analysts, 3D environment artists, historical researchers, and flight simulation developers, these coordinate-tagged LZW GeoTIFF tiles retain complete spatial positioning data for drag-and-drop alignment into QGIS, ArcGIS, Blender, and WorldEditor (WED).

🛠️ Technical Specifications & Integration Guide

Unlike standard compressed JPEG or PNG images, every tile inside this archive contains embedded georeferencing metadata tags. When imported into spatial editing software or game development engines, the tiles automatically map to their precise real-world geographic coordinates without manual scaling.

  • Tile Grid Spacing: 500 meters × 500 meters per individual raster segment.
  • Pixel Density: Rendered at native 104 DPI at a 1:500 spatial scale.
  • Compression Standard: Lossless LZW TIFF compression preserving full color and edge sharpness without artifacts.
  • Simulator Drag-and-Drop Note: Contains raw GIS data. X-Plane 12 developers can convert these tiles directly into .pol or .dsf terrain underlays using WED (WorldEditor) or Ortho4XP raster pipelines.
