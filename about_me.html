About the Developer

The Background

I am a qualified UAV engineer, geospatial specialist, and Earth observation researcher with a lifelong background in aviation, including experience as a former General Aviation student pilot.

Through OKB-1 Creative Design Bureau, I combine spatial data, remote sensing, and archival research to build period-accurate historical Digital Twins in X-Plane 12. My core mission is resurrecting forgotten aviation history—from Cold War military installations to vintage international airfields—with strict spatial precision.

Engineering Philosophy

Engineering logic meets digital art. My workflow prioritizes aero-performance and structural integrity over purely cosmetic meshes.

Every 3D asset, CAD model, and spatial polygon is built from first-principles using authentic blueprints, historical cartography, and real-world performance parameters to ensure absolute authenticity.

Core Specializations

  • Aerospace CAD & 3D Modeling: Parametric surface lofting and watertight geometry optimized for simulation and precision additive manufacturing.
  • Geospatial Analysis & GIS: Custom orthophoto raster processing and historical coordinate georeferencing via QGIS and GDAL.
  • X-Plane Scenery Architecture: Custom environmental integration, PBR ground layouts, and World Editor (WED) development.
