Berlin Tempelhof EDDI Restored (X-Plane 12)

How custom georeferenced orthophoto masking, legacy navdata recovery, and the iconic 3D crescent terminal brought Berlin's legendary aviation heartland back to life.

If you fire up default X-Plane 12 and point your aircraft toward Berlin Tempelhof (EDDI), you are met with a ghost town. In real-world history, Tempelhof was permanently decommissioned and transformed into the sprawling public Tempelhofer Feld, leaving default simulator installations with a closed 2D airstrip, crossed-out runway X-signs, and completely stripped navigation data.

At OKB-1 Creative Design Bureau, we refuse to let iconic aviation infrastructure remain dead on default simulators. Following our work on Johannisthal Field and Devau Airstrip, our latest alternate-history restoration project fully reactivates Berlin Tempelhof to an operational 2020 state — complete with high-resolution orthophoto underlays, active routing, and the majestic 3D crescent-shaped terminal building.

The Engineering Problem: The Tempelhofer Feld Paradox

When a massive urban airfield like Tempelhof closes in real life, default navdata databases wipe out its approach vectors and ground routing, leaving sim pilots constrained by automated database geometry:

Scenery Documentation & Installation Guide

To ensure X-Plane 12 loads your restored Tempelhof layout cleanly over default global assets, follow this installation hierarchy:

  1. Extract the project folder: Tempelhof-2020-Ortho.
  2. Move the entire folder into your X-Plane 12 Custom Scenery directory:
    X-Plane 12/Custom Scenery/Tempelhof-2020-Ortho/
  3. Launch X-Plane 12 once to update scenery_packs.ini, then close the simulator.
  4. Open Custom Scenery/scenery_packs.ini in any text editor and ensure the entry sits above default global libraries: 
    SCENERY_PACK Custom Scenery/Tempelhof-2020-Ortho/
SCENERY_PACK Global Scenery/Global Airports/
  5. Launch X-Plane 12 and enjoy flying to/from Tempelhof!

🛠️ Developer & WED Editing Notes

  • Exporting: Always use File -> Export Scenery Pack in WorldEditor (WED) to compile binary .dsf files.
  • Caching: If visual changes do not reflect immediately, use Ctrl + R in X-Plane to reload scenery.
  • Airport ICAO: Do not rename the internal ICAO declaration (EDDI) if you want this scenery to cleanly override the default Gateway layout.
